## Fixed

- **Magazine flickering during reloads and magazine checks.** With **Stick to magazine**,
  a magazine that was out of the gun could flicker in odd patterns or flash pale and blue.
  It was mostly visible outdoors in sunlight and went away indoors. The mod now hands the
  magazine back to the game's lighting cleanly every frame, so a magazine in your hand is
  lit exactly like the gun.
- **Camo slightly off on the magazine after a raid starts.** The mod could record where a
  gun's magazine sits while the gun was still on your back during loading, a few
  millimetres from where it sits in your hands. The magazine then never counted as seated
  and its camo was carried from the wrong spot. It now corrects that by itself within a
  moment of the gun being in your hands.

## Install

Extract into your SPT folder, replacing the old version. Requires Weapon Camo And Stickers 1.18.0 or newer.
